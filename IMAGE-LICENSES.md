## Destinations overview and guides (assets/img/guides/)

All 59 files in assets/img/guides/ were cropped from the seven desktop design mockups, plus the revised Jerash mockup (jerash-roman-road, jerash-theatre-view, jerash-lunch), supplied by Jordan Odyssey (overview, Aqaba, Wadi Rum, Petra, the Dead Sea, Jerash, Amman). The pages use these images.

- **Source and ownership:** supplied by Jordan Odyssey; treated as owned. **Confirm where the mockup photos came from, and that they may be used on the website, before launch.** Several look AI-generated rather than photographed at the real place.
- **Heroes (hero-*.jpg, plus a 640px version of each):** only the part of the mockup to the right of the baked-in title text. On desktop, the strip under the title is filled with the same photo, mirrored and softened, under the overlay. That fill is a stand-in until a clean source exists.
- **Bethany and Wadi Mujib:** the mockups had a "Nearby excursion" badge baked into the top-right corner. Those crops stop left of the badge, so a clean source is needed.
- **Resolution:** the crops are the mockup's own pixels, about 480px wide for cards and 760–1110px for heroes. They look soft on high-density screens and on wide monitors.

The files in assets/img/sights/ and assets/img/destinations/ listed below are no longer used by the pages.

## Other artwork

- **Logo** (`assets/img/brand/`): cut from the owner's chosen "Concept 03 — The Stone Inscriptions" image. The source is only 308 × 890 px, so a full-resolution logo file should replace these crops before launch.
- **Discover map:** the Jordan outline and the Dead Sea come from Natural Earth 1:10m data, which is public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/). `jordan-relief.svg` is decorative shading drawn for this site and is not survey data.
- **Nabataean lettering:** set in the Noto Sans Nabataean typeface (SIL Open Font Licence, no attribution on the page needed), not traced from any photograph.
  - Hero, 𐢛𐢚𐢓𐢈 (rqmw, "Raqmu", Petra's Nabataean name): M. Shaddel, *Journal of Semitic Studies* 62.2 (2017), 303–318.
  - Greeting, šlm ʾydʿn br zydw: OCIANA inscription CN.07-0001-02.26, https://ociana.osu.edu/inscriptions/25959.
