## Nabataean lettering sources

- **Hero, 𐢛𐢚𐢓𐢈 (rqmw, "Raqmu", Petra's Nabataean name):** M. Shaddel, "Studia Onomastica Coranica: al-Raqīm, Caput Nabataeae", *Journal of Semitic Studies* 62.2 (2017), 303–318, doi:10.1093/jss/fgx022. This is the source cited for the same spelling in Wikipedia's Petra article. The article says inscriptions spell the name *rqmw* or *rqm*.
- **Greeting section, 𐢝𐢑𐢒 𐢁𐢍𐢅𐢗𐢔 𐢃𐢛 𐢉𐢍𐢅𐢈 (šlm ʾydʿn br zydw, "Peace, ʾydʿn son of zydw"):** OCIANA, inscription CN.07-0001-02.26, ed. A. Al-Jallad and M. C. A. Macdonald, The Ohio State University, https://ociana.osu.edu/inscriptions/25959. It is the Nabataean half of a Nabataean–Hismaic bilingual inscription at Wadi Hafir, Aqaba Governorate, published in H. Hayajneh, *Proceedings of the Seminar for Arabian Studies* 39 (2009), 210–211. The letters are set in the Noto Sans Nabataean typeface and checked against their Unicode names. They are not traced from the stone.
