Every Stone Has a Story. We’re Here to Tell It.

Jordan isn’t a place you simply see. It’s a place you feel: the hush of the Siq before the Treasury appears, the silence of a Wadi Rum night, the warmth of a Bedouin welcome. Jordan Odyssey exists to help you experience all of it, without the stress of planning it alone.

Our story

Jordan Odyssey is a family-run team based in Jordan and the UAE. Our experience guiding travellers in Jordan shapes the way we plan each trip, from when to set out for Petra to where it’s worth staying an extra night.

If it’s your first visit, we’ll help you work out what fits comfortably into the time you have. If you’re travelling with children or grandparents, we can build in shorter drives, time for lunch, and an easier start after a long day. We’ll help you spend your time in Jordan in a way that suits the people you’re travelling with.

Why travel with Jordan Odyssey

  • First-hand local knowledge

    We draw on experience guiding in Jordan to suggest when to visit, where to find quieter viewpoints, and where to stop for a meal.

  • Designed for families

    We plan around comfortable transport, family-friendly stays, dining preferences, and a pace that works for everyone.

  • Every trip, your way

    Our programs are a starting point. We can adapt the dates, pace, hotels, and places you’d like to visit.

  • A real person to talk to

    You’ll hear directly from the team planning your trip.

  • Clear about what’s included

    We explain what the quote covers before you decide.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your trip

    Send an enquiry with your group size, what you’d love to see, and any dates you have in mind. It’s fine if you’re still deciding.

  2. Get your personal plan

    We’ll reply with a suggested itinerary and a clear quote for your dates and group.

  3. Refine your itinerary

    Once you’re happy with the itinerary, we’ll confirm what’s included and how each arrangement will be handled.
