Every Stone Has a Story. We’re Here to Tell It.

Jordan isn’t a place you simply see. It’s a place you feel: the hush of the Siq before the Treasury appears, the silence of a Wadi Rum night, the warmth of a Bedouin welcome. Jordan Odyssey exists to help you experience all of it, without the stress of planning it alone.

Our story

Jordan Odyssey is a family-run team. Our experience guiding travellers in Jordan shapes how we plan each trip, from when to set out for Petra to where it’s worth staying an extra night. If you’re travelling with children or grandparents, we can build in shorter drives, time for lunch and an easier start after a long day. Together, we plan journeys that feel personal from the first message to the last sunset.

Why travel
with Jordan
Odyssey

  • Local knowledge, first-hand

    Our plans are built by someone who guides in Jordan. We know the best time to enter Petra, the quieter viewpoints, and where locals actually eat.

  • Designed for families

    Sensible pacing, comfortable transport, family-friendly hotels and halal dining are part of the plan.

  • Every trip, your way

    Our programs are a starting point. Change the dates, the pace, the hotels or the places to visit, and we’ll shape the trip around you.

  • A real person, every step

    You talk directly with the people planning your trip, before you travel and while you’re there.

  • Clear and honest

    We explain what’s included before you commit.

How it works
