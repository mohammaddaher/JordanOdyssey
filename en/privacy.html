Privacy

How Jordan Odyssey handles the details you share with us, and what this website measures.

Last updated 17 November 2026

When you send an enquiry

The enquiry form sends us your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number if you add one, your trip details and your message. We use them only to reply to you and plan your trip. The form is delivered to our inbox by our email service, Resend. We don’t sell your details or share them for anyone else’s marketing.

Where your visit came from

Each enquiry also tells us how you found us, so we can see which pages and adverts bring travellers to Jordan Odyssey. This includes any campaign tags in the link you followed (such as utm_source or an advert click ID like gclid), the first page you landed on, the website that sent you, and the page you sent the form from. Your browser keeps the campaign details for up to 90 days, so an enquiry made on a later visit is still matched to the advert that first brought you here.

Google Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager, to understand how the site is used, for example which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are and which devices they use, and to measure our adverts. Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga to recognise returning visitors. You can read how Google uses this information on Google’s privacy site.

Your choices

If you visit from the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics and advertising cookies stay off until you choose Accept. Elsewhere they are on by default, and you can turn them off by choosing Reject. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

Your browser also stores three small items for this site: your cookie choice (kept for a year), the campaign details above (kept for 90 days) and the first page of your current visit.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Reply to any email from us, or send a message through the enquiry form.
