Privacy

How Jordan Odyssey handles the details you share with us, and what this website measures.

Last updated 17 November 2026

On this page

  • When you send an enquiry
  • How you found us
  • Analytics and cookies
  • Your choices

When you send an enquiry

When you fill in our enquiry form, you send us your name and email address, your phone or WhatsApp number if you add one, your trip details and your message.

We use these details only to reply to you and plan your trip. We don’t sell them or share them for anyone else’s marketing. The form reaches our inbox through our email service, Resend.

How you found us

Your enquiry may also tell us how you found us: the website or advert that brought you here, the first page you visited and the page you sent the form from. This shows us which pages and adverts are useful to travellers.

If you arrived through an advert or a campaign link, your browser keeps those details for up to 90 days. That way, an enquiry you send on a later visit is still matched to the advert that first brought you here.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use this website: which pages they visit, roughly where they are and what devices they use. It also helps us measure how well our adverts work.

Google Analytics uses cookies to recognise returning visitors. You can read how Google uses this information on Google’s privacy site.

Your choices

If you visit from the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics and advertising cookies stay off until you choose Accept. Everywhere else, they’re on by default, and you can turn them off by choosing Reject.

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page, or with the button below.

Your browser also keeps three small items for this website: your cookie choice (for a year), the campaign details mentioned above (for 90 days) and the first page of your current visit.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Just reply to any email from us, or send a message through the enquiry form.
