  1. Day 1

    Arrival in Amman

    Overnight AmmanAbout 35 km

    You’re met at Queen Alia International Airport (QAIA) and taken to your hotel in Amman.

  2. Day 2

    Amman to Petra

    Overnight PetraAbout 235 km

    After breakfast, head south to Petra, the rose-red city of the Nabataeans. Your guided tour starts with a horse ride to the Siq, then continues on foot to explore this World Heritage site. Visit the higher parts of the city before returning to your hotel.

  3. Day 3

    Petra, Aqaba and Wadi Rum

    Overnight Desert camp, Wadi RumAbout 180 km

    After breakfast, drive south to Aqaba on the shore of the Red Sea for half a day at the beach, with a chance to snorkel over the coral reefs. Then it’s a short drive north to Wadi Rum. Explore the desert on a 4×4 tour that ends at a sunset spot, where you watch the sun go down over a glass of Bedouin tea, then continue to a camp in the middle of the desert for dinner.

  4. Day 4

    Wadi Rum to the Dead Sea

    Overnight Dead Sea resortAbout 330 km

    After breakfast, leave Wadi Rum for the Dead Sea, the lowest point on Earth and its largest natural spa.

  5. Day 5

    Departure

    About 60 km

    After breakfast, transfer to Queen Alia International Airport (QAIA), with help through the departure formalities.
