  1. Day 1

    Arrival in Amman

    Overnight AmmanAbout 35 km

    You’re met at Queen Alia International Airport (QAIA) and taken to your hotel in Amman.

  2. Day 2

    Amman city tour, then the Dead Sea

    Overnight Dead Sea hotelAbout 70 km

    After breakfast, tour the Roman Theatre, the Citadel and the Umayyad Palace, with visits to the Archaeological and Folklore Museums. After free time to walk and shop in the old downtown, transfer to the Dead Sea, the lowest point on Earth, and spend the rest of the day at leisure.

  3. Day 3

    Dead Sea to Petra

    Overnight PetraAbout 230 km

    After breakfast, transfer to Petra, the rose-red city of the Nabataeans. Your guided tour starts with a horse ride to the Siq, then continues on foot to explore this World Heritage site. Visit the higher parts of the city before returning to your hotel.

  4. Day 4

    Little Petra, Wadi Rum and Aqaba

    Overnight AqabaAbout 200 km

    After breakfast, visit Al-Beida, known as Little Petra, where the Nabataeans received their guests. Then head south to Wadi Rum, Jordan’s largest desert and one of the most spectacular desert landscapes in the world, for a 4×4 tour that lasts until sunset, watched over a glass of traditional Bedouin tea. Continue to Aqaba.

  5. Day 5

    Aqaba to Amman

    Overnight AmmanAbout 310 km

    After breakfast and free time at leisure, transfer back to Amman.

  6. Day 6

    Departure

    About 35 km

    After breakfast, transfer to Queen Alia International Airport (QAIA), with help through the departure formalities.
